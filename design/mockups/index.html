CrossChess screens for the Xteink X3

1-bit PNGs at panel size (528×792 portrait, 792×528 landscape), drawn by design/mockups/render_mockups.py with the firmware's piece bitmaps and built-in fonts. UI text is Russian, the user's language. At 1× a pixel is a panel pixel; the panel is about 250 ppi, so it looks smaller on the device.
